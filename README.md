# Happy Diwali

A Diwali greeting that writes your name across the night sky in fireworks.

Open the page, type your name, and watch rockets rise from a rooftop terrace lit with diyas and burst into **Happy Diwali**, the year, and then your name, letter by letter. Add an emoji (🪔 ❤️ 🎉) and it gets its own little fireworks show in its own colours. When the show ends, the sky is yours: light any of 20 crackers whenever you like.

It works on phones, tablets, computers and TVs, in English, Hindi and Bengali.

## What you can do

- **See your name in fireworks.** Every word gets its own line, and each emoji gets its own scene.
- **Light crackers yourself.** Tap the sky to send up a rocket. Press and hold anywhere to open the cracker wheel, slide towards the one you want and let go.
- **Send a greeting to someone.** Write their name, your name and a short wish, and get a link (and a QR code) to share. When they open it, *their* name lights up the sky, followed by your wish and "with love from" you.
- **Choose the language.** Use the small **A | अ | অ** switch on the cards: the sky says *Happy Diwali*, *शुभ दीपावली* or *শুভ দীপাবলি*, with the year in matching numerals.
- **Scan a greeting.** Point your camera at a greeting's QR code, on a friend's phone, a TV or a printout, and it opens right away.
- **Save a video.** Record the whole show, sound included, and share it on WhatsApp or anywhere else.
- **Install it as an app.** Add it to your home screen. It opens full screen and works even without internet.

## The crackers

**On the terrace:** Anar (flowerpot), Chakra (ground spinner), Phuljhadi (sparkler), Ladi (string of crackers), Bijli bomb, Roman candle and Sutli bomb.

**In the sky:** Peony, Chrysanthemum, Willow, Palm, Crossette, Ring, Heart, Spiral, Crackle, Zig-zag rocket, Strobe, Multi-break and Sky lantern.

## How it feels real

The fireworks follow real physics: shells bloom, hang and droop in the breeze, sparks bounce off the floor, rockets wobble on their way up, and the letters of your name are made of real burning stars that settle into place, shimmer, and burn out. Smoke drifts across the sky, thin clouds glow in the colour of nearby bursts, the anars and diyas light up the terrace around them, and the booms arrive a moment after the flash, just like a real show far away.

## Using it

| To do this | On a phone or tablet | With a mouse or keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch a rocket | Tap the sky | Click the sky |
| Open the cracker wheel | Press and hold | Press and hold, or press `Q` |
| Mute or unmute | 🔊 button | 🔊 button or `M` |
| Replay the show | ↻ button | ↻ button or `R` |
| Send a greeting | Share button | Share button |
| Full screen | ⛶ button | ⛶ button or `F` |
| Change name, save a video, scan a QR code, install | ⋯ menu | ⋯ menu (`V` video, `C` scan) |

The full screen button only appears when it can help: the installed app on Android phones already opens full screen, so it is hidden there.

Sound is on from the start; you can mute it any time. People who prefer reduced motion get softer flashes and a steadier view.

## Your privacy

Everything happens in your own browser. The only things remembered are your own name, your language, the mute setting and your last chosen cracker, and they stay on your device. A name that arrives in a greeting link is never saved, so opening a friend's greeting does not replace your own name. The camera is used only while you are scanning, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Good to know

- Emoji look the way your device draws them, so they can differ a little between phones.
- When sending a greeting, each name can be up to 30 and the wish up to 50.
- The QR scanner uses [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR) (Apache License 2.0) on browsers that have no scanner of their own.
